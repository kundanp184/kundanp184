## About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student and an aspiring backend developer. I like understanding how systems work under the hood — how APIs are structured, how data moves between a database and a client, and how to build things that are reliable rather than just functional.

Right now, most of my energy goes into strengthening my backend fundamentals: writing cleaner APIs, understanding database design better, and improving my problem-solving through DSA. I've also taken part in a few hackathons, which taught me a lot about building under time pressure and shipping something that actually works.
